# NBA research: current state and handoff

A separate project from the NFL suite (`../nfl`): its own code (nothing imported from `nfl/`) and data cache (`nba/data_cache`, gitignored). Built November 2, 2026, with the NFL suite's standards: a rule is fixed on discovery seasons, then checked once on seasons no research step has seen, and judged by what it would have returned at real prices.

## Where it stands (November 2, 2026)

**Nothing here beats the NBA market at real prices, so there is no NBA betting run and no NBA card.** The models know something (their picks lose less than random bets), but not enough to clear the vig:

- **Spreads and totals** (section 3): on the untouched 2020-21 to 2025-26 seasons the correction model's pre-registered rules went **52.4%** on spreads (2,392-2,174) and **52.5%** on overs (1,033-935), against 52.38% break-even at -110.
- **Player props** (section 4): at the line every rule looked strong (56-73% won), but **at ESPN BET's own prices every one lost money** (-1.1% to -4.4% a bet). The book moved prices instead of lines, so what looked like an edge was already priced in. Value bets (only where the model's chance beats the price's implied chance) were negative for overs and between -1.2% and +1.7% a bet for unders: the best of 20 combinations scanned, not rising with the edge, and at a softer book than DraftKings. The props' untouched test seasons were left unspent.
- **The game-lines tracker** (section 5) is kept for occasional use: today's DraftKings spreads and totals with the model's leans, graded through the season. **Tracked, not bet.**

## 1. Data (all free; `nba_data.py`, `nba_lines.py`, `nba_prop_lines.py`, `nba_injuries.py`)

| Source | Content |
|---|---|
| sportsdataverse's ESPN NBA releases (`espn_nba_schedules`, `espn_nba_team_boxscores`, `espn_nba_player_boxscores`) | Every game since 1993-94: tip-off, venue, neutral site, scores; one row per team-game (shooting, rebounds, assists, turnovers, steals, blocks, fouls, paint and fast-break points); one row per player-game (minutes, box score, plus-minus, starter, did not play and why). Refreshed every morning about 7:40 AM Eastern; the latest season is downloaded again when asked (`refresh`), older ones come from the cache. CSV files, as this 32-bit Python has no parquet reader. |
| ESPN's odds archive (`sports.core.api.espn.com/.../events/{id}/competitions/{id}/odds`) | Every game's spread and total from several books since 2013-14 (5Dimes, BetOnline and Bovada early on; Caesars, DraftKings and others later), with prices and moneylines, and since 2023-24 the opening lines. `closing_lines` takes the median across sportsbooks, leaving out ESPN's aggregators and projection sites (numberfire posts zeros as placeholders; ESPN's own "consensus" sometimes has the spread's sign flipped), in-game "Live Odds" books and impossible values; the "Opening" provider (through 2016-17) is the opening line. Closing spreads miss the final margin by 9.1-10.9 points a season and totals by 13.4-14.9, and home teams covered 48-51%, so the lines are sound. Fill the cache with `python nba_lines.py history` (about 13 seconds a season). |
| ESPN's prop feed (`.../odds/{provider}/propBets`) | Player prop lines, current and opening: ESPN BET (provider 58) from late December 2023 to the end of November 2025, DraftKings (provider 100) from October 2026, nothing in between (the switch of books); about 100 player lines a game. ESPN BET's items say which side they are, with each side's price now and at the open; DraftKings' do not, and their order is not reliable (the over comes first about three times in four), so DraftKings lines can be graded only at the line. ESPN BET's lines were last updated after the games (settlement), so an ESPN BET line counts as the closing line, as in the NFL suite. `python nba_prop_lines.py history` fills the cache (`props_espn_<season>_v2.csv`, a few minutes a season). |
| Action Network's scoreboard (`api.actionnetwork.com/web/v2/scoreboard/nba`) | The day's DraftKings spreads and totals with their prices, the consensus, the opener and the betting splits (`nba_lines.day_lines`; Action Network keeps NBA lines back to late 2023). |
| ESPN's injury list (`site.api.espn.com/.../nba/injuries`) | Who is out tonight (Out, suspended, or ESPN's fantasy status OUT; the player's id is in his profile link); Day-To-Day players count as playing. |

ESPN's season numbers are the year a season ends (2027 is 2026-27). Teams are keyed by ESPN's id, which stays with a franchise through moves and renames; the 30 franchises are ids 1-30 (All-Star and exhibition teams have others).

## 2. The research table (`nba_lab.py`)

One row per regular-season game with everything known before tip-off, home minus away (`d_`) and home plus away (`s_`); `default_table()` caches it (about a minute to build from 2011-12 on).
- **Team ratings** (`rate_teams`): points per 100 possessions, the four factors, three-point rate and percentage, assists per basket, paint and fast-break points, steals, blocks, fouls, pace and margin, offense and defense. A recency-weighted average of earlier games (0.95 a game), each corrected by 0.6 of the opponent's deviation from the league average, shrunk toward the league mean by 5 pseudo-games, carried into a new season at 50%; the league mean drifts with scoring (about 100 to 115 points a team over these seasons). Pre-game ratings correlate 0.41 with the final margin (the closing spread 0.47), so nothing leaks from the game itself.
- **Context** (`add_context`): rest, back-to-backs, games in the 3 days before, travel and time zones since the last game, the arena's altitude, home or neutral.
- **Availability** (`availability`): a player's value is his recent Game Score per game played; a team's rotation is the players averaging 12+ minutes lately. Missing (rotation players who do not play), returning (rotation-calibre players back from an absence, new arrivals too) and playing. The backtests know who played (as the closing line mostly did). For games not played yet, the players who have played for the team this season and are not out on ESPN's injury list count as playing, which matches the backtests: a first version also counted players who had left the team and skewed every lean.

## 3. Spreads and totals (`nba_model.py`, `nba_research_validate.py`)

- **Model A** (stats only, ridge): misses the margin by 9.86 points (closing spread 9.61) and the total by 15.25 (closing total 14.20) on 2015-16 to 2019-20. A reference, as in the NFL.
- **Model B** (the correction model, ridge on standardized features): predicts the cover margin (`margin + home spread`) and the over margin (`points - total`) at the closing line, each season fitted only on earlier seasons from 2013-14.
- **The pre-registered validation** (written before any result was seen; `python nba_research_validate.py`, then `test`): discovery 2015-16 to 2019-20, untouched test 2020-21 to 2025-26. On discovery the spread correction correlated +0.060 with the cover margin (t 4.7, positive in all five seasons) and the total correction +0.048 with the over margin (t 3.7). The chosen rules: the side the spread correction favors by 0.5+ (53.5% of 3,859) and overs it favors by 1+ (54.0% of 341). On the test: correlations +0.049 (t 4.2) and +0.053 (t 4.5), so the information is real, but the rules went **52.4%** and **52.5%**, about break-even at -110. They cleared the letter of the bar set in advance (beat 52.38% with t >= 2) by a hair; that bar was too lenient, and they are not bet. A signal this size supports about 52-54% at any volume.

## 4. Player props (`nba_prop_lines.py`, `nba_prop_model.py`, `nba_research_props.py`)

- **The forecast** (`nba_prop_model.py`, as the NFL's): one gradient-boosted model (Poisson loss) per market (points, rebounds, assists, threes and the four combos; steals, blocks and turnovers left out in advance, as books price their 0.5-2.5 lines by moving the odds) from the player's recent and season production and minutes, per-minute rates, games just missed, his team's implied points, the spread, home, rest, both teams' ratings and the minutes and shots of rotation players out (his team's and the opponent's). Fitted on the 6 seasons before the one forecast; points forecasts correlate about 0.75 with the result. Two variants: **full** (who plays tonight, closing game lines) and **early** (only absences already known at the team's last game, opening game lines). The chance over or under a line comes from what happened after the nearest out-of-sample forecasts of the 4 seasons before.
- **The protocol** (written before any prop result was seen): discovery ESPN BET 2023-24 and 2024-25; untouched test ESPN BET October-November 2025 and DraftKings 2026-27; four families (full model at the closing line, overs and unders; early model at the opening line, overs and unders), each family's threshold chosen on discovery. Amended before any test: ESPN BET lines count as closing lines (their timestamps fall after the games), and each pick is also scored at its side's actual price.
- **Discovery results:** at the line, unders at the close won 56.8% (chance 0.60+, 34,183 picks), overs at the close 55.2%, and at the opening lines overs 72.8% and unders 70.7% (0.70+). **At the prices: -1.1%, -4.4%, -4.2% and -2.3% a bet.** The opening-line "edges" were stale openers the book had already priced against (Craig Porter Jr., a bench player starting for an injured Cleveland, opened at 20.5 points, moved to 13.5 and scored 7); the line later moved toward 79% of those overs. Closing-price value bets (`python nba_research_props.py value`): overs -2.4% to -5.6% at every edge, unders -1.2% to +1.0%; opening-price value bets: overs -0.6% to -2.2%, unders -0.1% to +1.7%. Not proposed for betting, and the test seasons were left unspent for a better idea.

## 5. The game-lines tracker (`nba_prod_games.py`)

For occasional use, by hand: `python nba_prod_games.py` from this folder (the packages are the NFL suite's, `requirements.txt`). It rebuilds the research table with the latest box scores and ESPN's injury list, fits Model B (spreads alpha 1000, totals alpha 10000) and Model A on every completed season, takes DraftKings' current lines for today's games from Action Network, and writes `<Desktop>\NBA\<season>_nba_games.xlsx` (the `NBA_FOLDER` environment variable points it elsewhere): **Today** (the games left to tip off, their lines, the spread lean when the correction favors a side by 0.5+, the over lean at 1+, and Model A's fair spread), **Season Log** (every game tracked, keeping the line and lean of the last run before tip-off, graded once final) and **Model Info** (the research). About two minutes a run. Early in a season the leans run larger (ratings are still settling and rosters have changed), as they did in the backtests.

## 7. If this is picked up again

Ideas not tried, each needing its own pre-registered test (the props' untouched seasons are still unspent):
- **DraftKings' prices by side** from another source, so value bets can be checked at the book actually used.
- **Timing:** a snapshot log of lines through the day would show whether any lean moves the line (closing-line value), which the archive cannot (it keeps the open and the close only).
- **Narrower markets:** first-half lines, team totals, or props in the minutes right after injury news, where books lag; this data has no timestamps fine enough to test them.
